📋 Setup Checklist

1

Add Your 2nd Google Account

Sign out in the Antigravity 2.23.0 app (or via terminal), sign in with your 2nd Google AI Pro account, and snapshot the profile.

A. Sign Out Current Account
agy /logout

Or in the Antigravity App: click Settings (gear icon in lower-left) → "Sign Out".

B. Log In with 2nd Google Account
agy /login

Complete the browser sign-in prompt with your 2nd account.

C. Snapshot Profile as google_2
.\tools\account_router\ai_switch.ps1 save google_2
2

Add Your 3rd Google Account (If You Have One)

Repeat the exact same flow for Account 3, then switch back to Account 1:

agy /logout
agy /login
.\tools\account_router\ai_switch.ps1 save google_3
.\tools\account_router\ai_switch.ps1 1
3

Daily Workflow in Antigravity App 2.23.0

Whenever you hit a usage limit or want to switch accounts while using the desktop client:

Action 1: Switch Account

Run a single numeric command in PowerShell:

.\tools\account_router\ai_switch.ps1 2

✓ Swaps OAuth tokens & App storage in <10ms

Action 2: Reload App Window

Click the Antigravity App window and press:

Ctrl + R

✓ Re-binds UI & language server instantly
